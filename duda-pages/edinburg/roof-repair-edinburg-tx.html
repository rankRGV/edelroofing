Roof repair services in Edinburg

Leak repair

Ceiling stains, active drips, or damp insulation. We trace the leak to where water actually enters, which is often several feet from where it shows up inside, and repair it there.

Storm and hail damage repair

Missing or lifted shingles, granule loss, and impact damage from Hidalgo County storms. We document what we find in writing, which helps if you file an insurance claim.

Flashing repair

Flashing around chimneys, vents, and valleys is one of the most common leak sources on Edinburg homes. We reseal or replace failed flashing so water runs off the way it should.

Shingle repair by section

Cracked, curling, or wind torn shingles are replaced in the affected area and matched as closely as possible to your existing roof.

Skylight and vent repair

Failed skylight seals and vent boots are a common, specific leak source. We repair or reseal them without disturbing the rest of the roof.

Inspections after storms

A full walk of the roof after hail or high wind, with a written report on what needs repair now and what to keep an eye on.

What causes most roof repairs in Edinburg

Heat and constant sun wear down shingles faster here than in cooler parts of the country. Shingles that look fine from the street can already be brittle. In older neighborhoods near downtown, many repair calls start with a small ceiling stain that turns out to be failed flashing or a cracked vent boot that has been dripping slowly for a long time.

On the north side, in newer subdivisions, hail and wind are the more common cause. One storm can lift a section of shingles or dent the surface enough to let water in, and the homeowner needs both the repair and the paperwork for the insurance company.
