What types of commercial roofs do you install in Edinburg?

We install TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen membrane systems — the most common flat and low-slope commercial roofing systems in South Texas. We also apply roof coatings for buildings that need extended life without a full replacement. We can recommend the right system for your building type, roof area, and budget.

How long does a commercial roof last in the Rio Grande Valley?

A well-installed commercial membrane system typically lasts 15 to 25 years in our climate, depending on the material and how well it is maintained. Heat and UV exposure shorten the lifespan compared to cooler regions. Regular inspections can catch small issues before they shorten that timeline significantly.

Do you work with property managers and building owners, not just business owners?

Yes. A significant part of our commercial work is with property managers overseeing multiple buildings and building owners who lease to tenants. We can work around tenant schedules, provide documentation for insurance and maintenance records, and set up recurring inspection agreements.

Can you help with an insurance claim for hail or storm damage to a commercial roof?

We can inspect the damage and give you a written report of what we find. That documentation is useful when you file a claim. We do not act as a public adjuster, but we can walk you through what we observed so you understand what to discuss with your insurance company.
