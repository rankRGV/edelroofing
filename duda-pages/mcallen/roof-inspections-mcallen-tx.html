Roof inspections for McAllen homeowners and buyers

A roof inspection tells you what's actually going on up there, not what it looks like from the ground. We walk the roof, check the flashing, vents, and valleys, and look for anything that's wearing out or already failed. Then we give you a written report and a straight answer — not a sales pitch dressed up as an inspection.

Most people call us for one of three reasons: they're buying a house and want to know what they're getting, they just had a storm come through, or they've noticed something and want a professional opinion before it becomes a bigger problem.

When McAllen homeowners get a roof inspection

Buying a Home

Know the real condition of the roof before you close. We tell you the age, remaining life, and anything that needs attention now versus later.

After a Storm

Hail and wind damage aren't always visible from the ground. A post-storm inspection catches problems before they turn into leaks.

Something Looks Off

A stain on the ceiling, a missing shingle you spotted from the yard, a roof that's getting up there in age. We check it and tell you what we find.

Selling a Home

A written inspection report before you list can head off a buyer's inspection surprise, and gives you room to fix small issues on your own timeline.

What we find most often in McAllen

McAllen's housing stock splits pretty cleanly by age. Homes around 2nd Street and 10th Street were mostly built in the 1980s and 90s, and a lot of those original shingle roofs are now due — or overdue — for replacement. If you're buying in one of these older neighborhoods, an inspection tells you exactly where that roof stands.

Out toward Sharyland and Las Cumbres, the homes are newer and the roofs are younger, so what we usually find there is storm-related — a section of shingles from a wind event, a flashing issue around a vent, something targeted rather than a whole-roof problem.

Roof inspection questions — McAllen

What does a roof inspection actually cover?

We check the shingles or membrane, flashing, vents, valleys, and the general condition and remaining life of the roof. You get a written summary of what we found, not just a verbal opinion.

How long does an inspection take?

Most residential inspections take 30 to 45 minutes on-site. We'll walk you through what we found before we leave, and follow up with the written report.

Is the inspection really free, or is there a catch?

It's free, no obligation. If we find something that needs repair or replacement, we'll give you a written estimate — but there's no pressure to move forward with it.

Should I get a roof inspection before buying a house in McAllen?

Yes, especially for homes built before the 2000s. Roof age and condition are one of the biggest hidden costs in a home purchase, and a $8,000-$18,000 replacement is worth knowing about before you close, not after.
