Roof coatings for McAllen businesses

A roof coating is a reflective layer applied over an existing flat or low-slope roof. It's not a replacement — it's a way to extend the life of a roof that's still structurally sound but starting to show wear, while cutting down on the heat the building absorbs through the roof.

Coatings make the most sense on commercial buildings with TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen roofs that have some life left but are aging. If the underlying roof is already failing, a coating won't fix that — we'll tell you honestly if replacement is the better call instead.

What a roof coating does

Extends Roof Life

A properly applied coating adds years of service life to a roof that's aging but still sound, delaying the cost of a full replacement.

Reduces Heat Load

Reflective coatings bounce back sunlight instead of absorbing it, which can lower interior temperatures and cooling costs during McAllen summers.

Seals Minor Wear

The coating process seals small cracks and seams in the existing membrane, adding a layer of protection against water intrusion.

Lower Cost Than Replacement

When the roof qualifies, a coating costs a fraction of a full tear-off and replacement — worth evaluating before committing to a bigger job.

Where coatings make sense in McAllen

McAllen has a lot of flat and low-slope commercial roofing — the strip centers along Nolana Ave, medical office buildings near South Texas Health System, and warehouse and industrial properties in north McAllen. Roof coating questions — McAllen

What is a roof coating?

It's a reflective liquid-applied layer put over an existing flat or low-slope roof. It seals minor wear, reduces heat absorption, and extends the roof's usable life without a full tear-off.

Is a coating right for my roof?

It depends on the roof's current condition. If the underlying membrane is still structurally sound but showing age, a coating usually makes sense. If the roof is already failing or leaking extensively, replacement is the better long-term answer. We'll give you a straight assessment during the inspection.

How long does a roof coating last?

Depending on the product and the condition of the roof underneath, a coating can add several years of service life. We'll give you a realistic estimate specific to your roof, not a generic number.

Is a coating cheaper than a full roof replacement?

Yes, when the roof qualifies. A coating costs a fraction of a tear-off and replacement, which is why it's worth evaluating first on a roof that's aging but not failed.

Do you apply coatings to residential roofs too?

Coatings are most common on flat and low-slope commercial roofs. Most residential roofs in McAllen are pitched shingle roofs, where a coating isn't the right fit — call us and we can tell you what does apply to your specific roof.
