What roofing problems are most common in McAllen?

McAllen has a mix of roof types that comes with being a large, fast-growing city. Older neighborhoods around 2nd Street and 10th Street have homes with shingle roofs from the 1980s and 90s that are due for replacement. North McAllen, around the Sharyland and Las Cumbres areas, has newer homes where storm damage and repairs are more common than full replacements.

On the commercial side, the strip centers along Nolana Ave, the medical buildings near South Texas Health System, and the warehouse and industrial properties in north McAllen all have flat or low-slope roofs. A flat roof leak during business hours is a real problem. We can usually get an inspection on the calendar quickly.

The RGV climate is hard on roofs. Extreme heat accelerates shingle degradation and membrane wear. UV exposure breaks down materials faster here than in cooler regions. When a McAllen hailstorm comes through, the damage can range from cosmetic to structural. An inspection after any significant storm is worth doing.
