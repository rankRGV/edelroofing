Storm damage inspection and repair in McAllen

After a hailstorm or high wind event, roof damage isn't always obvious from the ground. Missing granules, bruised shingles, and lifted flashing can go unnoticed for months until they turn into a leak. We inspect the whole roof — not just the parts you can see from your driveway — and tell you honestly what we find.

If the damage is real, we document it in writing with enough detail to support an insurance claim. If it's cosmetic and doesn't affect the roof's performance, we tell you that too. We're not paid more for finding damage that isn't there.

What a storm damage inspection covers

Hail Impact

Bruising, granule loss, and cracked shingles from hail impact. We check the whole roof, not just the sides that face the street.

Wind Uplift & Missing Shingles

High wind can lift or tear shingles even when nothing looks obviously wrong from below. We check every edge, ridge, and valley.

Flashing & Vent Damage

Storms move flashing and knock loose the seals around vents and skylights — a common source of leaks that shows up weeks later.

Written Insurance Documentation

Photos and a written report of exactly what we found, useful when you file a claim. We're not a public adjuster, but we'll walk you through what we observed.

Storm damage questions — McAllen

How do I know if I have hail damage on my roof?

Hail damage often isn't visible from the ground — bruised shingles and granule loss show up close-up, not from your driveway. If there was a hailstorm in your area, it's worth getting a free inspection even if nothing looks wrong yet.

Do you help with insurance claims for storm damage?

We inspect the roof and give you a written report with photos documenting what we find. That documentation is useful when you file a claim. We're not a public adjuster, but we'll walk you through what we observed so you know what to present to your insurer.

How soon should I get an inspection after a storm?

Sooner is better. Some insurance policies have a filing window after a storm event, and damage that isn't causing a leak yet can worsen with the next rain. A free inspection costs you nothing either way.

Does storm damage always mean I need a full roof replacement?

No. Sometimes it's a targeted repair — a few shingles, some flashing, a resealed vent. We recommend replacement only when the damage or the roof's age actually calls for it, not by default.
