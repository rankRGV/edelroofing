Why McAllen roofs need replacing sooner than you'd think

Extreme heat and constant UV exposure age a shingle roof faster in the RGV than in most of the country. A 20-year shingle in McAllen often behaves more like a 15-year shingle by the time it's actually inspected. That's what we see most often in the older neighborhoods around 2nd Street and 10th Street — homes with roofs original to the 1980s and 90s construction, well past their realistic service life even if they don't look dramatically bad from the street.

In the newer areas around Sharyland and Las Cumbres in north McAllen, replacements are more often storm-driven — hail or wind damage across enough of the roof that patching stops making financial sense compared to a full replacement covered by insurance.

Either way, we walk the whole roof before recommending replacement, and we tell you plainly if a repair would actually do the job.

How do I know if my McAllen roof needs replacing instead of repairing?

Widespread granule loss, multiple leak points, a roof over 20-25 years old, or storm damage across a large section of the roof are the usual signs. We inspect the whole roof and decking and give you an honest recommendation, not an automatic upsell to replacement.

How much does a roof replacement cost in McAllen?

For a typical McAllen home, a full shingle replacement generally runs between $8,000 and $18,000, depending on roof size, pitch, current material, and shingle grade. We give a written estimate before any work begins.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements in McAllen take one to three days depending on roof size and complexity, weather permitting. We give you a specific timeline as part of the written scope before work starts.
