Roof repair services in McAllen

Leak Repair

Interior water stains, active drips, or damp insulation. We trace the leak to its actual entry point — which is often several feet from where the water shows up inside — and repair it there.

Storm & Hail Damage Repair

Missing or lifted shingles, granule loss, and impact damage from Hidalgo County storms. We document what we find in writing, which is useful if you're filing an insurance claim.

Flashing Repair

Flashing around chimneys, vents, and roof valleys is one of the most common leak sources on McAllen homes. We reseal or replace failed flashing so water sheds the way it's supposed to.

Shingle Replacement (Sections)

Cracked, curling, or wind-torn shingles replaced in the affected area, matched as closely as possible to your existing roof.

Skylight & Vent Repair

Failed skylight seals and roof vent boots are a common, specific leak source. We repair or reseal them without disturbing the rest of the roof.

Post-Storm Inspections

A full walk of the roof after a hailstorm or high-wind event, with a written report on what needs repair now and what to keep an eye on.

What causes most roof repairs in McAllen

McAllen's heat and UV exposure wear down shingles faster than in cooler climates — shingles that look fine from the ground can already be brittle underneath. That's why a lot of our repair calls in older neighborhoods near 2nd Street and 10th Street start with a homeowner noticing a small stain on the ceiling that turns out to be a flashing or vent issue that's been dripping slowly for a while.

In the newer subdivisions around Sharyland and Las Cumbres in north McAllen, storm damage is the more common trigger — a hail event knocks loose a section of shingles or dents the roof enough to compromise the surface, and the homeowner needs both a repair and documentation for their insurance company.

Either way, the fix starts with an honest inspection. We tell you what we actually see, not what generates the biggest invoice.
