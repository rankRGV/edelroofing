What is a fair price for a roof repair in Mission?

Most repairs run a few hundred dollars up to around $1,500, depending on what is wrong and how much of the roof is affected. A fair price is one backed by a written estimate after an actual inspection, not a phone-quote guess. We give you that estimate before any work starts.

What is the average cost to repair a roof in Texas?

Statewide, small repairs like flashing or a section of shingles typically fall between $300 and $1,500. Larger repairs involving decking or a bigger damaged area can run higher. The Valley's heat adds a variable other parts of the state do not deal with as much — UV exposure ages shingles faster, so a repair that looks minor can be sitting on brittle material underneath.

What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It is a code-driven guideline some jurisdictions and insurers apply: if more than 25% of a roof section needs replacing, the whole section (sometimes the whole roof) has to be brought up to current code rather than patched. It matters most on older Mission homes where a "small" repair area can trip that threshold once we actually get up there and measure it.

Will insurance cover a 20-year-old roof in Texas?

It depends on the policy and the cause of damage. Wear-and-tear from age is usually not covered, but a specific event — hail, wind, a storm — often is, even on an older roof. We inspect and document what we find so you have something concrete to bring to your insurance company; we are not a public adjuster, but we can tell you honestly whether what we see looks like an insurable event or ordinary aging.
