Yes. We repair flat and low-slope commercial roofs throughout Mission. Flat roof leaks are harder to trace than residential leaks because water travels along the membrane before it shows up inside. What types of commercial roofing do you install in Mission?

We install TPO, EPDM, and modified bitumen membrane systems — the three most common flat and low-slope commercial systems in South Texas. How long does a commercial roof last in Mission's climate?

A well-installed commercial membrane system in the RGV typically lasts 15 to 25 years, depending on the material and how consistently it is maintained. Mission's heat and UV exposure accelerate wear compared to cooler climates. Regular inspections catch small problems before they shorten that timeline.
