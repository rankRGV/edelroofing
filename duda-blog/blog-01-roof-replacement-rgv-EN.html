A simple guide for homeowners in Edinburg and the Rio Grande Valley

A roof replacement is a full removal of your old roof down to the decking, followed by a new roof system installed from scratch. Most homeowners in Edinburg and the Rio Grande Valley need one when their roof is worn out from age, storm damage, or repeated leaks that repairs no longer fix. This guide covers how to know when it's time, what it costs, which materials hold up in our climate, and what the process looks like from inspection to cleanup.

How do you know when you need a roof replacement?

You likely need a roof replacement if you see one or more of these signs:

  • The roof is 20 years or older. Most asphalt shingle roofs in the RGV last 15 to 25 years. Once a roof reaches that age, replacement is usually more cost-effective than ongoing repairs.
  • Shingles are curling, cracking, or missing. This means the shingles have lost their protective layer and can no longer keep water out.
  • You find granules in your gutters. The sandy granules that wash off shingles are a sign the roof is breaking down.
  • You have repeat leaks. If you have patched the same area more than once, the problem is usually the roof system, not a single spot.
  • There are sagging areas or soft spots. This points to water damage in the decking underneath and needs attention right away.
  • Daylight shows through the attic. If light comes through, so does water.

If you are not sure, the fastest way to know is a free inspection. A roofer can tell you whether a repair will hold or whether replacement is the better choice.

How long does a roof last in the Rio Grande Valley?

A typical asphalt shingle roof in the Rio Grande Valley lasts about 15 to 25 years. Metal roofs last longer, often 40 years or more. Our climate is hard on roofs for a few reasons:

  • Heat and UV. Long, hot summers break down shingles faster than in cooler regions.
  • Hail and wind. Storms across Hidalgo and the surrounding counties can damage a roof in a single afternoon.
  • Humidity. Moisture speeds up wear and can lead to mold or rot if the roof is not sealed correctly.

Because of this, roofs here often need replacement closer to the lower end of their expected lifespan than roofs in milder parts of the country.

Should you repair or replace your roof?

Repair makes sense when the damage is small, recent, and limited to one area — for example, a few shingles blown off in a storm on an otherwise healthy roof. Replacement makes sense when the roof is old, when damage covers a large area, or when leaks keep coming back after repairs.

A simple way to decide: if the cost of repairs is approaching a large share of the cost of a new roof, or if you are repairing the same roof every year, replacement is the better long-term value.

How much does a roof replacement cost in the Rio Grande Valley?

The cost of a roof replacement in the Rio Grande Valley depends on a few factors:

  • The size of your roof — measured in squares (one square equals 100 square feet).
  • The material — asphalt shingle costs less than metal.
  • The pitch and complexity — steep or multi-level roofs take more labor.
  • The condition of the decking — if the wood underneath is damaged, it has to be replaced too.

What roofing materials work best in the RGV heat?

The two most common choices for homes in the Rio Grande Valley are:

  • Asphalt shingles. The most common and most affordable option. Good shingles handle our heat well and come in many colors. This is the right choice for most homes.
  • Metal roofing. Costs more up front but lasts longer and reflects heat, which can lower your cooling bills in the summer. A strong option if you plan to stay in your home long term.

EDEL Roofing installs both and can help you choose based on your budget, how long you plan to stay, and the look you want. Energy efficiency matters here — a roof that reflects heat instead of holding it can make a real difference on your electric bill during a Valley summer.
