Wondering what a fair roof repair price actually looks like in Mission? Most repairs run a few hundred dollars up to around $1,500 — but the honest number only comes after we inspect, not from a phone quote.

There's also a code rule worth knowing: if more than 25% of a roof section needs fixing, that section has to be brought up to current code instead of just patched. It matters most on older Mission roofs.

And on insurance: a storm or hail event is often covered even on an older roof. Ordinary wear from age usually isn't. We inspect and document what we find either way.
