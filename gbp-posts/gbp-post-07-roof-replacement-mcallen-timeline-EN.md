Thinking about a roof replacement in McAllen? Two questions come up every time: how long will it take, and will insurance help pay for it?

Most residential replacements here run one to three days, depending on roof size and whether the decking underneath needs repair once the old shingles come off.

Insurance is a different story. It typically covers replacement when the cause is a specific event — hail, high wind, storm damage. It doesn't cover a roof that's just old and worn out from years of Valley heat.

We inspect first, give you a written estimate and timeline, and document anything storm-related in writing for your insurance company.
