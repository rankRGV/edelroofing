Finding #1 · Fix This First
No conversion tracking is set up

The campaign is running on a "Maximize clicks" bid strategy — meaning Google is optimizing purely for getting people to click, not for getting you leads. There's no way, right now, to tell which of the 2,098 clicks turned into an actual customer.

Why this matters more than anything else on this page: Every other recommendation below is a guess until this is fixed. Once conversion tracking is live (calls, form fills, or both), the bid strategy can switch to actually optimizing for leads instead of clicks — same budget, better targeting.

Finding #3 · Worth a Conversation
$68.87 went to your own business name

"Edel roofing" was the single highest-spend search term in the account — 13 clicks, $68.87. Edel Roofing already ranks #1 organically for this exact term (confirmed live in the SEO side of this engagement), which means people searching your name would likely find you for free anyway.

Some agencies keep branded spend running deliberately — it protects the top spot from a competitor bidding on your name. Worth a quick decision either way, not a clear-cut fix.
