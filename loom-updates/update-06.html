Step 1 · Done
Google verified your new address

Google confirmed 1815 N Jackson Rd, Edinburg, TX 78541 as the verified address on your Business Profile on September 11. Your website header and footer already showed it. While closing this out we found two technical listings on your site (the structured data Google reads to confirm who you are), and one of them still had the old Alberta Rd address. We fixed it and checked it live the same day. Your site and your Google profile now show the same address everywhere Google looks.

Two different addresses on one site can confuse Google while it is trying to confirm a business. That is no longer a risk.
Step 2 · In progress
Citation cleanup is underway

A citation is any listing of your business name, address, and phone number on another website. Many of yours still show the old phone number or the old address. We are starting with the sources that matter most: Apple Maps, Bing, and the large data aggregators that feed information to dozens of smaller directories. About 50 new citations are also lined up to go out soon.

This is in progress, not finished. Each directory has its own review process, so corrections take days to weeks to show up. We will report how many are confirmed live at the next update.
