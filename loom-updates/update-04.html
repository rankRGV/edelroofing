Step 2 · The Measurement We Promised
We re-ran the AI test. It has not moved yet.

In June we asked ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity five real customer questions to see whether any of them would recommend Edel. Four of the five went well. One failed on every platform: "I have a flat roof leak on my business in McAllen TX, who can fix it?" Zero out of four. That is the question the McAllen commercial page was built to answer, and we told you we would re-test once the page was live.

June 24 · Before the page
  • 0 of 4 platforms mentioned Edel for this question
  • No McAllen commercial content existed
July 29 · After the page
  • 0 of 20. We asked each platform five separate times to be sure.
  • ChatGPT 0/5 · Claude 0/5 · Gemini 0/5 · Perplexity 0/5
  • The page is live and indexed. The AI answers have not caught up.
What we make of it. These tools build their answers largely from what already ranks well and what gets talked about across the web. The McAllen pages are two weeks old and do not rank yet, so it follows that the AI answers do not know about them yet either. The honest read is that it is too early, not that the approach is wrong. The same thing happened in Edinburg first, where you now get recommended on most questions. What we will not do is tell you this moved when it has not.
