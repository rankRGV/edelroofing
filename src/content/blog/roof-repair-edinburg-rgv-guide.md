## How to tell if your roof needs a repair, what it costs, and what to expect

Most roof problems in Edinburg and the rest of the Rio Grande Valley start small. A shingle lifts after a storm. A slow drip shows up only when it rains. You find a patch of granules in the gutter. The question is whether a repair will do or whether the roof needs to be replaced. This guide covers how to tell the difference, what roof repair costs in the RGV, and what happens when a roofer comes out.

## Signs your roof needs a repair

These are the most common reasons homeowners in Edinburg and across Hidalgo County call us for a roof inspection:

- **A leak after rain.** Water on a ceiling or an inside wall means something on the roof has failed. The leak is almost never right above the stain, because water runs along the decking and rafters before it drips. A good inspection traces it back to where the water actually gets in.
- **Missing or lifted shingles.** Wind pulls shingles loose at the edges and ridges first. A few missing shingles don't mean the roof is done. They mean that section is open and needs fixing before the decking underneath gets wet.
- **Damaged flashing.** Flashing is the metal strip that seals the spots where the roof meets a chimney, vent pipe, skylight or wall. When it pulls away or corrodes, water gets in along those seams. Most flashing problems are repairs, not replacements.
- **Granules in the gutters.** Some granule loss is normal, especially on an older roof. A lot of it, like gutters full of granules after a hailstorm, means the shingles took real impact damage and may be losing their weather protection faster than they should.
- **Cracked or curling shingles in one area.** If the damage stays in one section and the rest of the roof looks sound, that section may be repairable without touching the whole roof.

## Repair vs replacement: how to decide

It depends on how old the roof is, how much of it is damaged, and how much life is left in the parts that aren't damaged.

A repair makes sense when the damage is in one area, the rest of the roof has several good years left, and the decking underneath is dry. A full replacement makes more sense when the roof is more than 20 years old, when the damage is spread across a big part of the roof, or when the inspection finds the decking has gone soft from moisture.

Honestly, you can't know for sure until someone walks the roof and checks the decking. An inspection takes about 30 to 45 minutes and shows you the condition of the whole roof, including the parts you can't see from the ground.

## How much does roof repair cost in the Rio Grande Valley?

The cost depends on what failed. These are rough ranges for the most common jobs around Edinburg and McAllen:

- **Shingle repair (small section):** $250 to $600 to patch a few damaged or missing shingles.
- **Flashing repair or replacement:** $200 to $500, depending on how many penetrations are involved and whether the seal underneath needs work.
- **Leak diagnosis and patch:** $300 to $700. That covers finding where the water actually gets in, beyond the damage you can see, and sealing it.
- **Ridge cap replacement:** $500 to $1,200 for a one story home. Ridge caps take the most wind and are often the first part of the roof to fail.
- **Larger section repair (significant storm damage):** $1,000 to $3,500, depending on the area and the material.

These are estimates, not quotes. To get a real number, someone has to inspect your roof. We give written estimates before any work begins.

## Does homeowner's insurance cover roof repairs?

It depends on what caused the damage. Insurance usually covers repairs after sudden events like a hailstorm, high winds or a falling tree branch. It doesn't cover wear from age and normal weather, which insurers call a maintenance issue.

If you think a storm caused your damage, call your insurance company and ask about filing a claim before you have any work done. We can inspect the roof and write up what we find. That report is useful when you talk to your adjuster. We're not a public adjuster and we don't handle the claim for you, but we can go over what we saw with you.

## What happens during a roof repair in Edinburg?

Here's how it usually goes:

1. **Inspection.** A licensed roofer walks the roof, checks the decking where we can reach it, and notes what needs to be repaired. If a storm caused the damage, we put the findings in writing.
1. **Written estimate.** Before any work starts, you get a written estimate showing what we'll do and what it costs. No surprise charges later.
1. **Repair.** Most home repairs are done in one day. Bigger jobs, like larger sections, several penetrations or decking work, may take two days.
1. **Cleanup.** We clear the debris and run a magnetic sweep around the work area for loose nails.
1. **Documentation.** For insurance work, we can give you written documentation of the finished repair.
