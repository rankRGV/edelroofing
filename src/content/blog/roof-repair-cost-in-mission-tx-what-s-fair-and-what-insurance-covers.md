### What a fair price looks like, what the 25% rule means, and whether insurance will pay

When a Mission homeowner calls us about a roof repair, the same two questions come up almost every time. Is this price fair? Will insurance help pay for it? Both have real answers. Here's what sets the price and what decides the coverage.

## What is a fair price for a roof repair in Mission?

Most repairs cost a few hundred dollars up to about $1,500, depending on what's wrong and how much of the roof is affected. Fixing one piece of flashing or a small patch of shingles is at the low end. A repair that covers a wider area, or one where we find soft decking once we're up there, costs more.

A fair price comes with a written estimate after someone has actually looked at your roof. A number quoted over the phone, before anyone has been up there, isn't one. We walk the roof first, tell you what we find, and give you the estimate in writing before any work starts.

## What's the average cost to repair a roof in Texas?

Across the state, small repairs like flashing, a section of shingles or a vent boot usually cost between $300 and $1,500. Bigger repairs that involve the decking or a larger damaged area cost more than that. Mission has something a lot of the state doesn't deal with as much: steady heat and UV exposure age shingles faster here than almost anywhere else in the country. A repair that looks minor from the ground can be sitting on material that's already brittle underneath. That's part of why we inspect before we quote instead of pricing off a description.

## What is the 25% rule for roofing?

It's a code based guideline that some jurisdictions and insurers apply. If more than 25% of a roof section needs replacing, that whole section, and sometimes the whole roof, has to be brought up to current code instead of patched. It can change the size of a job. On older Mission homes, especially the shingle roofs from the 1970s and 80s near downtown and Conway Avenue, a repair that looks small can cross that 25% line once we measure it. When that happens, we tell you before work starts, not after.

## Will insurance cover a 20 year old roof in Texas?

It depends on the policy and on what caused the damage. Age alone doesn't decide it. Normal wear from age usually isn't covered, because insurers treat it as maintenance. Damage from a specific event, like hail, wind or a storm, often is covered, even on a roof that's 20 years old, because the damage is tied to an incident and not to slow wear.

That difference matters in Mission. The older homes near Conway Avenue and downtown are more likely to have plain wear from age, which is a harder case to make with an insurer. The newer homes toward Sharyland are more likely to have a real storm damage claim, even though the roof is younger. We inspect the roof and write down what we find, so you have something concrete to bring to your insurance company. We're not a public adjuster and we don't handle the claim, but we'll tell you straight whether what we see looks like storm damage or normal aging.

1. **Documentation, if it applies.** If the damage looks like it came from a storm, you get our findings in writing to take to your insurance company.
