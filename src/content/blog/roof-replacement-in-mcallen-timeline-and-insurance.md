### How long a replacement takes, whether insurance covers it, and what to expect in McAllen

Once a McAllen homeowner decides to replace the roof, two questions come up almost every time. How long will it take? Will insurance cover any of it? Both answers depend on your house, but there are real ranges worth knowing before a crew shows up.

## How long does a roof replacement take in McAllen?

Most home roof replacements in McAllen take one to three days from tear off to cleanup, weather permitting. Where your job lands in that range comes down to a few things:

- **Roof size and pitch.** A one story home with a simple gable roof goes faster than a steep roof with several levels, valleys and dormers.
- **Decking condition.** If the tear off shows water damaged decking, that section gets repaired or replaced before the new roof goes on. This is the most common reason a one day job turns into two.
- **Material.** A standard asphalt shingle replacement goes faster than a switch to metal, which takes more custom flashing work.
- **Weather.** Rain stops a replacement. A torn off roof can't sit open when a storm is coming in. During McAllen's summer rainy stretches, a job can move a day or two around the forecast.

New construction and bigger homes in the newer subdivisions around Sharyland and Las Cumbres can run toward the longer end simply because the roofs are bigger. Older homes near 2nd Street and 10th Street often have smaller footprints. But if the decking under them has been soaking up moisture for a decade, that's where the timeline stretches.

## Does insurance cover a roof replacement in McAllen?

Sometimes, and it depends on the cause. Homeowners insurance generally covers a roof replacement when the damage comes from a sudden, specific event: a hailstorm, high winds, a tree limb that falls on the roof. It does not cover a roof that is simply old and worn out from years of heat and sun. Insurers see that as maintenance, not a covered loss.

That difference matters a lot in McAllen. The newer homes around Sharyland and Las Cumbres are more likely to have a storm claim. Hail damages a roof that still had plenty of life left, and insurance pays for some or all of the replacement. The older homes near 2nd Street and 10th Street are more often past their expected lifespan from age alone. That means a claim is less likely to hold up, even when the roof clearly needs replacing.

If you think a storm caused the damage, call your insurance company before any work starts. We can inspect the roof and give you written documentation of what we find, and that report helps when you talk to your adjuster. We're not a public adjuster and we don't handle the claim, but we'll walk you through exactly what we saw.

## What to expect, start to finish

1. **Inspection.** We walk the whole roof and check the decking, not only the section that looks bad from the ground. If storm damage might be involved, we write it up.
1. **Written estimate.** You get a specific price and a specific timeline before any work begins. No surprise charges once the crew is on the roof.
1. **Tear off.** The old roofing comes off down to the decking. This is when hidden problems like soft spots and water damage actually get found.
1. **Decking repair, if needed.** Damaged decking gets replaced before the new roof goes on. This step is what most often turns a one day estimate into two.
1. **Installation.** New underlayment and shingles go on, installed to the manufacturer's spec.
1. **Cleanup.** We haul off the debris, run a magnetic sweep for loose nails and leave the site clean.
