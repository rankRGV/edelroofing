A roof replacement means taking your old roof off down to the decking and installing a new roof system from scratch. Most homeowners in Edinburg and the Rio Grande Valley need one when the roof is worn out from age, storm damage, or leaks that keep coming back after repairs. This guide covers how to know when it's time, what it costs, which materials hold up in our climate, and how the job goes from inspection to cleanup.

## How do you know when you need a roof replacement?

You probably need a roof replacement if you see one or more of these signs:

- **The roof is 20 years or older.** Most asphalt shingle roofs in the RGV last 15 to 25 years. Once a roof gets to that age, replacing it usually costs less in the long run than paying for repair after repair.
- **Shingles are curling, cracking or missing.** That means they've lost their protective layer and can't keep water out anymore.
- **You find granules in your gutters.** The sandy granules that wash off shingles are a sign the roof is breaking down.
- **You have repeat leaks.** If you've patched the same area more than once, the problem is usually the roof system, not one spot.
- **There are sagging areas or soft spots.** This points to water damage in the decking underneath and needs attention right away.
- **Daylight shows through the attic.** If light gets in, so does water.

If you're not sure, a free inspection is the fastest way to find out. A roofer can tell you whether a repair will hold or whether replacement is the better choice.

## How long does a roof last in the Rio Grande Valley?

A typical asphalt shingle roof in the Rio Grande Valley lasts about 15 to 25 years. Metal roofs last longer, often 40 years or more. Our weather is hard on roofs. Long, hot summers and strong UV break down shingles faster than in cooler places. Hail and wind storms across Hidalgo and the nearby counties can damage a roof in one afternoon. Humidity speeds up wear and can lead to mold or rot if the roof isn't sealed correctly.

That's why roofs here often need replacing closer to the low end of their expected lifespan than roofs in milder parts of the country.

## Should you repair or replace your roof?

A repair makes sense when the damage is small, recent and in one area, like a few shingles blown off in a storm on a roof that's otherwise in good shape. A replacement makes sense when the roof is old, when damage covers a large area, or when leaks keep coming back after repairs.

One simple way to decide: if your repair bills are getting close to a large share of what a new roof costs, or you're fixing the same roof every year, replacement is the better value over time.

## How much does a roof replacement cost in the Rio Grande Valley?

The cost of a roof replacement in the Rio Grande Valley depends on a few things:

- **The size of your roof**, measured in squares (one square is 100 square feet).
- **The material.** Asphalt shingles cost less than metal.
- **The pitch and layout.** Steep roofs and roofs with several levels take more labor.
- **The condition of the decking.** If the wood underneath is damaged, it has to be replaced too.

Because of all this, no single price fits every home. The only way to get an accurate number is to measure and inspect the roof in person. EDEL Roofing gives you a free estimate with no pressure, so you know the real cost before you decide anything.

## What roofing materials work best in the RGV heat?

The two most common choices for homes in the Rio Grande Valley are:

- **Asphalt shingles.** The most common and most affordable option. Good shingles handle our heat well and come in a lot of colors. This is the right choice for most homes.
- **Metal roofing.** Costs more up front, but it lasts longer and reflects heat, which can lower your cooling bills in the summer. A good option if you plan to stay in your home for a long time.

EDEL Roofing installs both and can help you choose based on your budget, how long you plan to stay and the look you want. In a Valley summer, a roof that reflects heat instead of holding it can help with your electric bill.
